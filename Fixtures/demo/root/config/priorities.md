# What counts as urgent

Urgent items reach the owner within minutes. Everything else waits for the next batch.

## Urgent
- A buyer or supplier waits on a price, a quantity or a delivery date.
- A deadline falls today: a payment, a filing, a signature.
- Family asks for help.

## Normal
- Offers and counteroffers with more than a day to answer.
- Questions from tenants and agents.

## Low
- Bookings and appointments more than three days out.
- Anything the owner can answer with a single word next week.
